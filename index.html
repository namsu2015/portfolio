Publications

T-SAR: A Full-Stack Co-design for CPU-Only Ternary LLM Inference via In-Place SIMD ALU Reorganization

H. Oh, K. Nam, R. Bhattacharjya, H. Chen, T. Das, S. Yun, S. Jang, A. Ding, N. Dutt, M. Imani

Design, Automation & Test in Europe Conference & Exhibition (DATE), 2026

Contribution. Performed gem5 cycle-accurate simulations and ALU-level ternary-operator analysis used in the paper's performance–energy evaluation of CPU-based ternary inference. Analyzed architectural implications of mapping ternary computation onto CPU SIMD datapaths with limited hardware modification.

Results. T-SAR reported 5.6–24.5× GEMM latency reduction and up to 86.2× GEMV throughput improvement over an NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin GPU, with approximately 3.2% power and 1.4% area overhead in a TSMC 28nm implementation.

PolyQ: Codesigning End-to-End Quantization Framework for Scalable Edge CPU LLM Inference

H. Oh, S. Jang, H. Chen, K. Nam, S. Yun, R. Masukawa, M. Imani

International Conference on Computer-Aided Design (ICCAD), 2026, accepted

Contribution. Contributed to theoretical review and technical discussion of mixed-precision quantization for CPU-oriented low-bit LLM inference.

Results. PolyQ combines quantization with system-level optimization for efficient CPU execution, reporting 2.4–32.1% improvement in 3-bit perplexity and up to 70.8% reduction in runtime activation reorder traffic.
